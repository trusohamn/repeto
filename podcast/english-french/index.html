Repeto – I learn French

Learn French by listening and speaking, with English prompts to guide you. Repeto is an audio course for beginners of all ages: children, parents, grandparents and anyone who wants to start learning French. No previous knowledge or textbook is needed.

How it works: you hear a word or sentence in English, then have a pause to say it in French. Next, you hear the answer twice, with time to repeat it and practise the pronunciation.

What's included:

  • Season 1 – Practice: 30 short topics, from greetings and numbers to food, feelings and everyday situations.
  • Season 2 – Story: "The Magical Radio" (La radio magique), one chapter per episode. Listen to the chapter in French, practise its vocabulary, then listen to the chapter again.
  • More stories are on the way. Follow the podcast to hear new episodes.

How to use the course:

  • Start with season 1, episode 1 and listen in order.
  • Answer out loud during each pause. Try to recall the words yourself.
  • Replay an episode until you can answer before the recorded voice.
  • Listening in the background can help you get used to the sounds and rhythm of the language. Practise actively too, by repeating and answering during the pauses.

Enjoy this format? Repeto is also available in other languages, with the same familiar structure. Search for Repeto in your podcast app.

Get in touch! Suggestions for new languages, corrections and story ideas are welcome: repeto.lingo@gmail.com

Voices are AI-generated (Microsoft Azure).
