How it works

Every episode works the same way

  1. Pick a course

    Choose the language you speak and the one you want to learn.

  2. Press play

    Start with episode 1. You don't need to look at the screen after that.

  3. Say it

    You hear a phrase in your language. Say it in the new language during the pause.

  4. Check and repeat

    The answer is played twice. Check what you said and say it again.

What's inside

Two ways to learn

Phrases

Everyday phrases

30 everyday topics, such as greetings, food, weather and feelings. Each phrase follows this pattern:

I'm hungry.… your turn …Tengo hambre.
Stories

The Magical Radio

The first story, in eight chapters. Each episode plays a chapter, practises its key words, then plays the chapter again. More stories are coming.

Who it's for

For children and adults

  • Screen-free

    Nothing to look at, so you can listen while walking, driving or cooking.

  • Free, no ads

    No account, no tracking on this site, nothing to pay for.

  • Printable transcripts

    Every episode has a transcript you can read or print.

  • Beginner-friendly

    Starts from zero and repeats each phrase several times. No reading needed.
