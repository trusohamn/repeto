About this course

Learn German by listening and speaking, with English prompts to guide you. Repeto.stream is an audio course for beginners of all ages: children, parents, grandparents and anyone who wants to start learning German. No previous knowledge or textbook is needed.

How it works: you hear a word or sentence in English, then have a pause to say it in German. Next, you hear the answer twice, with time to repeat it and practise the pronunciation.

What's included:

  • Phrases: 30 short topics, from greetings and numbers to food, feelings and everyday situations.
  • Story: "The Magical Radio" (Das magische Radio), one chapter per episode. Listen to the chapter in German, practise its vocabulary, then listen to the chapter again.
  • More stories are on the way. Follow the podcast to hear new episodes.

How to use the course:

  • Start with the first phrase episode and listen in order.
  • Answer out loud during each pause. Try to recall the words yourself.
  • Replay an episode until you can answer before the recorded voice.
  • Listening in the background can help you get used to the sounds and rhythm of the language. Practise actively too, by repeating and answering during the pauses.

Enjoy this format? Repeto.stream is also available in other languages, with the same familiar structure. Search for Repeto.stream in your podcast app.
